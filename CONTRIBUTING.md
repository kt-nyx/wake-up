# Contributing

Keep changes focused on a demonstrated improvement or compatibility problem.
Describe the behavior changed, supported versions, fallback behavior and the
checks you actually ran. Successful compilation is not a gameplay or speed claim.
See [the build instructions](docs/source-build.md) and [architecture](docs/architecture.md).

By submitting a contribution for inclusion, you license your original software
under GPL-3.0-or-later with the permission in LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md, and original
prose/artwork under CC BY-SA 4.0. You retain copyright. You also agree to the
separate [Workshop contribution terms](WORKSHOP_CONTRIBUTION_TERMS.md).

Sign off each commit to certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](DCO).
Use `git -c core.hooksPath=.githooks commit -s`. The sign-off records your
certification; it is not a copyright assignment. The pull-request template
separately records platform consent.

Do not include game files, decompiled research, mod binaries, private saves,
logs containing private data, or another person's implementation without its
license and exact provenance. Reference an independently installed dependency
where possible. Third-party imports require a file-level review; their existing
licenses cannot automatically acquire this project's linking permission.

AI-assisted work follows the same requirements. Explain material assistance
when relevant to review, inspect the result yourself, and do not claim rights
or authorship that you cannot certify.
